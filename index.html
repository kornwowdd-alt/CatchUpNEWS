How CatchUp NEWS Works

Four simple steps from raw footage to published TikTok video.

1

Connect TikTok

Authorize your creator account via TikTok Login Kit with basic profile and posting scopes.

2

Upload & Preview

Select your 9:16 vertical video (.mp4 or .webm) and preview the playback before publishing.

3

Configure Post

Set captions, hashtags, privacy levels, and interactions (Comments, Duets, Stitches).

4

Instant Publish

Click publish to transfer binary video chunks directly to TikTok servers with real-time feedback.
