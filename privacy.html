Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Panda Creator Studio ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share when using our application in connection with TikTok APIs.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize Panda Creator Studio via TikTok Login Kit, we only access the specific scopes you explicitly grant:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Your TikTok Open ID, display name, and avatar URL to display your connected account in the publishing interface.
  • Content Publishing Permissions (video.publish, video.upload): OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to query creator publishing settings and upload or publish media you select.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your session and display your creator profile information before publishing.
  • Upload and publish video or photo content to your TikTok account according to the privacy and interaction settings you explicitly choose.
  • Maintain and refresh your OAuth authorization session so you can manage your publishing workflow seamlessly.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • OAuth tokens are stored securely and used strictly for executing API requests initiated by the authorized user.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or TikTok OAuth tokens with any third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • You may disconnect your TikTok account or revoke access at any time via the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • Upon revocation or request, any stored tokens and cached profile metadata associated with your account are promptly deleted.

5. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with the TikTok for Developers platform. Your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact the administrator via our official support channel.
