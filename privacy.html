Official Application Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026 | Effective Date: October 12, 2026

CatchUp NEWS ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our web application collects, uses, processes, and safeguards user data when you interact with our platform and integrate your TikTok account via TikTok for Developers APIs.

1. Information We Collect Through TikTok APIs

When you authorize CatchUp NEWS using TikTok Login Kit, we only request and access the specific OAuth permissions (scopes) that you explicitly consent to:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): We receive your TikTok Open ID, public display name (nickname), and avatar URL. This information is utilized solely to personalize your publishing dashboard and confirm which TikTok account is currently connected.
  • Content Posting Capabilities (video.publish, video.upload): We receive short-lived OAuth access tokens and long-lived refresh tokens that allow our application to initialize post uploads, query creator settings (allowable privacy levels and interaction settings), and publish or upload your designated video content to your TikTok account.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We use the data strictly for providing and improving the publishing services of CatchUp NEWS:

  • To verify and maintain your authenticated session.
  • To display your connected creator identity and active publishing configuration.
  • To transmit your video files and metadata (title, hashtags, privacy levels, and comment/duet/stitch preferences) to the official TikTok Content Posting API endpoints upon your explicit action.
  • To automatically refresh your OAuth access tokens to maintain uninterrupted publishing workflow.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CatchUp NEWS does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or TikTok OAuth credentials to third parties or advertising brokers. Your data is only shared with:

  • TikTok Platform: Media and authorization tokens are communicated directly to official TikTok API servers (open.tiktokapis.com) to execute publishing requests you command.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to secure your data. OAuth tokens are encrypted and transmitted over secure HTTPS protocols. We never store or ask for your TikTok password.

5. Data Retention, Revocation, and Deletion

  • Revocation: You can disconnect CatchUp NEWS and revoke all permissions at any time directly through the TikTok mobile application (navigate to Settings and Privacy → Security and Permissions → Apps and Services).
  • Deletion Request: You may request complete deletion of any stored tokens or cached profile data associated with your account by sending an email to support@catchupnews.com. Upon receipt, all associated data will be permanently purged within 30 business days.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

Our Service integrates with the TikTok for Developers platform. Your interactions with TikTok are independently governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
